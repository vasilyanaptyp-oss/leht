Where MALCHEM coatings are used

Coatings for metal that has to withstand weather, load and chemicals.

Can you paint today?

If the steel surface is too close to the dew point, moisture forms on it and the coating does not adhere properly. The general rule: the surface temperature must be at least 3 °C above the dew point.

Enter the air temperature, relative humidity and surface temperature. The result is indicative; exact requirements are in each product's technical data sheet.

01Services

What we do

Electrical works in shipbuilding: installation and commissioning, measurements and distribution switchboards.

  1. F1

    Electrical installation

    Electrical installation works on board: cable laying, connections and mounting of electrical equipment.

  2. F2

    Commissioning

    Start-up and adjustment: we put the installed electrical systems into operation and set them up.

  3. F3

    Measurements

    Electrical measurements of installations and equipment on site.

  4. F4

    Distribution switchboards

    Manufacturing of distribution switchboards for your project.
